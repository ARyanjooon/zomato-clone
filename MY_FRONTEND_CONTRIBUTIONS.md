# My Frontend Contributions

Project: zomato-clone

## My role
I focused primarily on the frontend experience, creating the UI and client-side flows for discovering restaurants, browsing menus, and navigating the ordering journey.

## Work completed
- Built the landing page and restaurant listing experience
- Designed filter and search interactions
- Implemented reusable food and restaurant cards
- Created responsive layouts for different screen sizes
- Improved product polish and the visual hierarchy of key actions

## Challenges and solutions
- Problem: creating a content-rich interface that still felt clean and easy to navigate
- Solution: used a clearer visual hierarchy, reusable cards, and simplified filtering controls
- Problem: making the experience usable on smaller screens
- Solution: designed with a mobile-first approach and responsive layout tweaks

## What to highlight in interviews
- My approach to product-style frontend design
- How I structured reusable UI components
- My focus on usability and responsive product experiences
